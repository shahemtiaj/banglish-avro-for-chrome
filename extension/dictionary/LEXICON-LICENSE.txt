lexicon.txt — Bangla word list
==============================

lexicon.txt in this folder is derived from the dictionary of Avro Phonetic
(avrodict.js, as distributed with ibus-avro: https://github.com/sarim/ibus-avro).

Changes made for SE Banglish Avro: the word list was filtered (archaic verb
forms and entries with non-letter characters removed), a small number of
everyday words were added, and every word was grouped under a "sound key".
The script that performs this conversion is scripts/build-lexicon.mjs in
https://github.com/shahemtiaj/banglish-avro-for-chrome.
